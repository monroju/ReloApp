Questions

How much income do I need to move abroad from the US?

Of the visas here, the lowest bar is the Portugal D7 Visa at €920 a month for the applicant. The highest personal-income bar is the Mexico Permanent Resident Visa.

Do these thresholds include my spouse and children?

No. They are for the main applicant. Each visa adds an amount per family member, listed next to it.

Which exchange rate does the calculator use?

The European Central Bank reference rate published on 2026-10-26: 1 US dollar = 0.8938 euros. Rates move every day, so leave yourself a margin if you are close to a threshold.

Does any income count?

No. Retiree and passive-income visas usually exclude salary, and digital nomad visas need income from remote work. Check the basis line under each visa.
