Golden & Investment Visas for Americans (2026)

Each visa in detail

🇵🇹 Golden Visa — Portugal

Income: €500k fund / cultural — real estate excluded since 2023 · Processing: 6-12 months · Cost: €500k investment + ~€10k fees
Path to PR: 5 years · Citizenship: 5 years

Pros

  • Only 7 days/yr presence required
  • Family included
  • Citizenship in 5yr

Trade-offs

  • Real estate option killed Oct 2023
  • Long AIMA processing
  • Large capital lockup

City guides · Official source

🇮🇹 Investor Visa — Italy

Income: €500k Italian co / €2M bonds / €1M philanthropy · Processing: 30-90 days · Cost: €500k+ investment + €500 visa
Path to PR: 5 years · Citizenship: 10 years

Pros

  • No physical presence required
  • Fast Nulla Osta
  • Family included

Trade-offs

  • High capital lockup 2yr min
  • Italian tax residency triggers
  • Approval not guaranteed

Official source

🇭🇺 Guest Investor Programme — Hungary

Income: €250k real estate fund / €1M property · Processing: 1-3 months · Cost: €250k+ investment + ~€100k fees
Path to PR: 10yr Guest Investor residence · Citizenship: 8 years

Pros

  • Long 10yr residence card
  • Family included
  • Schengen access

Trade-offs

  • Relaunched 2024 — process new
  • Large capital lockup
  • Investment options limited

Official source

🇦🇷 Investor Visa — Argentina

Income: ARS-denominated business investment · Processing: 3-6 months · Cost: Investment + ~$500
Path to PR: 2 years · Citizenship: 2 years from PR

Pros

  • Work in own business
  • Fast naturalization (2y)
  • Mercosur passport

Trade-offs

  • Investment must hold in ARS (inflation)
  • Business plan scrutinized
  • Argentine bureaucracy
