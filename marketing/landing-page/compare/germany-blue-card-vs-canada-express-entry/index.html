Germany Blue Card vs Canada Express Entry (2026)

The two most-searched skilled-migration routes for Americans, one into the EU and one into Canada. Germany's EU Blue Card is employer-tied and needs a qualifying salary and a job offer; Canada's Express Entry is a points-based pool that doesn't require a job offer at all. The Blue Card can be faster if you already have an offer; Express Entry is more flexible if you don't. Here's how they compare.

The details

🇩🇪 EU Blue Card — Germany

Income: €45,300+/yr (€41k shortage occupations) · Processing: 4-8 weeks · Cost: €100 fee
Path to PR: 21mo (B1 German) / 33mo (A1) · Citizenship: 6-8 years

Pros

  • Fastest PR pathway in EU
  • Family open work permit
  • Schengen-wide travel

Trade-offs

  • University degree required
  • Salary bar high in non-shortage roles
  • German bureaucracy

🇨🇦 Express Entry (Skilled Worker) — Canada

Income: Points-based (CRS) — age/education/work · Processing: ~6 months · Cost: ~CAD $1,500 in fees
Path to PR: Immediate · Citizenship: 3 years

Pros

  • Fastest PR pathway
  • No employer required (FSW)
  • Family included

Trade-offs

  • CRS cutoffs rose sharply 2024
  • English/French test required
  • Backlog on category-based draws
