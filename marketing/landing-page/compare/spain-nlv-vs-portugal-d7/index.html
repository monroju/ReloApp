Spain NLV vs Portugal D7: Which Is Right for You? (2026)

This is the single most-asked question among Americans retiring or living passively in Iberia. Both give you a path to an EU passport. The real differences: Portugal's D7 needs far less income and lets you work, while Spain's NLV forbids all local work but offers a more established expat infrastructure. Here's the honest side-by-side.

The details

🇪🇸 Non-Lucrative Visa — Spain

Income: ~€2,400/mo passive (4× IPREM, +25% per dependent) · Processing: Up to 3 months (legal max) · Cost: $153 consular fee (US) + lawyer optional
Path to PR: 5 years · Citizenship: 10y (2y Latin Am/Sephardic)

Pros

  • No employer needed
  • Family included (+25% IPREM per dependent)
  • Path to EU passport

Trade-offs

  • No work in Spain at all
  • Renewal scrutinizes time outside Spain
  • Costly health insurance required

🇵🇹 D7 Passive Income Visa — Portugal

Income: ~€920/mo (€11,040/yr) passive · Processing: 4-6 months · Cost: €110 visa + lawyer ~€1,500
Path to PR: 5 years · Citizenship: 10 years (7 for CPLP/EU)

Pros

  • Lowest income bar in Europe
  • Permanent residence after 5 years
  • Work allowed in Portugal

Trade-offs

  • Slow AIMA processing
  • 183-day/yr presence required
  • NHR tax regime ended Mar 2024
