Spain DNV vs Portugal D8: Digital Nomad Visa Comparison (2026)

Both let a remote worker or freelancer live in Iberia on foreign income, and both lead to an EU passport. The decision usually comes down to tax: Spain's Beckham Law offers a flat 24% for new arrivals, while Portugal's post-NHR IFICI is narrower. Income bars and client rules differ too. Here's how they stack up.

The details

🇪🇸 Digital Nomad Visa — Spain

Income: ~€2,650/mo from non-Spanish employer (+75% spouse / +25% per child) · Processing: 20-45 days (fast track) · Cost: €100 fee + lawyer optional
Path to PR: 5 years · Citizenship: 10y (2y Latin Am/Sephardic)

Pros

  • Beckham Law tax regime 24% flat
  • Apply in-country as tourist
  • Family included (+75% spouse, +25% per child)

Trade-offs

  • Must prove 3yr employment history
  • Cannot have >20% Spanish clients
  • Hacienda audit risk

🇵🇹 D8 Digital Nomad Visa — Portugal

Income: ~€3,480/mo (4× Portuguese minimum) · Processing: 4-6 months · Cost: €90 visa + lawyer ~€1,500
Path to PR: 5 years · Citizenship: 5 years

Pros

  • Citizenship in 5yr
  • Family included
  • EU residency rights

Trade-offs

  • 4× higher income bar than D7
  • AIMA backlog 9-18mo
  • No NHR tax benefit anymore
