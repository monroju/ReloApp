Best Digital Nomad Visas for Americans (2026)

These visas are built for people earning from a foreign employer or their own clients. The differences that matter: the monthly income bar, whether there's a favorable tax regime, and how long you can actually stay. Spain's Beckham Law and Hungary's hard two-year cap sit at opposite ends of that spectrum.

Each visa in detail

🇪🇸 Digital Nomad Visa — Spain

Income: ~€2,442/mo from non-Spanish employer (consulate); ~€2,849 often asked in Spain. +75% first family member, +25% each extra · Processing: 10 business days (consulate) / 20 days (in Spain) · Cost: $190 consular fee (US) + lawyer optional
Path to PR: 5 years · Citizenship: 10y (2y Latin Am/Sephardic)

Pros

  • Beckham Law tax regime 24% flat
  • Apply in-country as tourist
  • Family included (+75% first member, +25% each extra)

Trade-offs

  • Must prove 3yr employment history
  • Cannot have >20% Spanish clients
  • Hacienda audit risk

🇵🇹 D8 Digital Nomad Visa — Portugal

Income: ~€3,680/mo (4× Portuguese minimum wage) · Processing: 4-6 months · Cost: €110 visa + lawyer ~€1,500
Path to PR: 5 years · Citizenship: 10 years (7 for CPLP/EU)

Pros

  • Permanent residence in 5yr
  • Family included
  • EU residency rights

Trade-offs

  • 4× higher income bar than D7
  • AIMA backlog 9-18mo
  • No NHR tax benefit anymore

🇮🇹 Digital Nomad Visa — Italy

Income: ~€24,789+/yr (3× health-cost exemption) · Processing: 30-120 days · Cost: €116 visa + ~€120 permit + lawyer optional
Path to PR: 5 years · Citizenship: 10 years

Pros

  • Launched Apr 2024, fast track
  • Spouse/kids included
  • 5% flat tax regime for high-skilled

Trade-offs

  • Must prove 'highly skilled' (degree or 5yr exp, 3 for ICT)
  • Income bar varies by consulate
  • Tax residency complex

🇲🇽 Temporary Resident (Remote Work) — Mexico

Income: ~$4,300/mo from foreign employer · Processing: 4-8 weeks · Cost: $56 consulate + ~$600 INM card
Path to PR: 4yr → Permanent Resident · Citizenship: 5y from first resident card

Pros

  • No Mexican client restriction
  • Low tax burden if non-resident <183d
  • Family included

Trade-offs

  • Same income bar as Econ Solvency
  • Cannot work for Mexican company
  • Strict 4yr cap on Temp

🇭🇺 White Card (Digital Nomad) — Hungary

Income: €3,000/mo from foreign employer · Processing: 30-90 days · Cost: ~€100
Path to PR: Cannot lead to PR — must switch tracks · Citizenship: Cannot lead to citizenship directly

Pros

  • Easy entry to Central Europe
  • Tax residency at 15% flat
  • Schengen access

Trade-offs

  • No path to PR or citizenship
  • Max 2yr total
  • Cannot bring spouse on White Card
