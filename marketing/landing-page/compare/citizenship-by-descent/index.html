Get a Second Passport by Ancestry: 2026 Comparison

A second passport by ancestry is the cheapest and most durable way abroad, because it's recognition of citizenship you may already hold, not a visa you can lose. The catch is proving the paper trail. Eligibility rules differ sharply, and some (Italy, UK) changed recently, so verify current rules before assuming you qualify.

Each visa in detail

🇮🇹 Citizenship by Descent (Jure Sanguinis) — Italy

Income: No income test · Processing: 1-3 years · Cost: €600+ + research costs
Path to PR: Skip — instant EU citizenship · Citizenship: Immediate

Pros

  • EU passport without moving
  • No language test for descent claims
  • Children automatically eligible

Trade-offs

  • 2025 reform: parent/grandparent with only Italian citizenship (DL 36/2025)
  • Consulate backlogs years long
  • 1948 maternal-line cases need lawsuit
