Hungary Simplified Naturalization 2026: Ancestry Route Guide

Free to apply, no residency requirement, children included — Hungary's ancestry route looks like every other EU citizenship-by-descent option on paper. Then there's the embassy interview, conducted in Hungarian.

What Simplified Naturalization is

Hungary offers a streamlined citizenship path for people who can document a Hungarian ancestor, without requiring residency in Hungary at any point. It's aimed squarely at the diaspora — descendants of Hungarians who emigrated, particularly around the World Wars and 1956.

The requirement that sets this apart: the language interview

This is the single biggest practical difference between Hungary's ancestry route and the Irish, Italian, or Polish equivalents on this list: none of those require the applicant to actually speak the ancestral language. Hungary does. You'll need to demonstrate basic conversational Hungarian in an embassy interview as part of the process — factor language study into your timeline the same way you'd factor in document gathering.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • No residency requirement at any point
  • Children of a successful applicant are eligible
  • No government application fee — the cheapest of any ancestry route on price alone

Cons

  • Basic conversational Hungarian is a hard requirement, verified in an embassy interview
  • Embassy backlogs commonly stretch to the long end of the 6–12 month window
  • Documenting the ancestral chain can be as rigorous as any other EU descent route
