Ireland Stamp 0 Retiree Visa 2026: Complete Guide for Americans

Stamp 0 lets financially independent people live in Ireland indefinitely without ever working. It's also a dead end if what you actually want is an Irish passport. Know which one you're signing up for before you apply.

What Stamp 0 is — and isn't

Stamp 0 is a residency permission, not an immigration pathway. It's designed for people who can support themselves entirely on passive income and have no intention of working in Ireland or naturalizing. It's commonly used by financially independent retirees, people who already have family living in Ireland, and people who need a caregiver to accompany them into the country.

If your goal is eventually holding Irish or EU citizenship, Stamp 0 will not get you there — plan around a different visa (or an ancestry route, if you have Irish grandparents — see our Foreign Births Register guide) if citizenship is the end goal.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Legal, long-term residency in an EU member state
  • Low physical-presence requirement compared to many residency permits
  • No obligation to work — by design, not a workaround

Cons

  • The €50,000/year bar is high relative to other passive-income routes on our list
  • Never leads to permanent residency or citizenship, no matter how many years you renew it
  • Working is prohibited outright — not "limited," prohibited

Who this actually fits

Be honest with yourself about the trade here: Stamp 0 is a good fit if you want to live in Ireland long-term and never touch naturalization paperwork again — for example, retirees with a pension well above the threshold, people joining family already resident in Ireland, or someone accompanying a family member as a caregiver. It's a poor fit if your actual goal is an EU passport; that ambition needs a different route entirely.
