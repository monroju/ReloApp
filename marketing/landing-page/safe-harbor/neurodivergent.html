Best Countries for Neurodivergent Americans Moving Abroad

Adult ADHD and autism diagnosis pathways, medication continuity, and English-speaking clinical communities differ sharply between countries. Tiers weigh public-system access, waitlist reality, and advocacy infrastructure.

Updated June 2026 · 11 countries assessed

Strong fit

Law, services and community all line up.

🇩🇪 Germany

Adult ASD/ADHD diagnosis via SPZ (sozialpädiatrische Zentren) covered by GKV; waitlists 6–12 months. Strong autism research base; Berlin and Munich have English-speaking ND clinical networks.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom

NHS adult ASD/ADHD diagnosis waitlists exceed 36 months in most areas; Right to Choose lets you select an independent provider on NHS funding (~6 months). Strong ND advocacy culture.

Good, with caveats

Workable — read the note for the catch.

🇨🇦 Canada

Provincial coverage for adult ASD/ADHD diagnosis varies — Ontario covers via OHIP psychiatrists (6–18 month wait); BC similar. Vancouver and Toronto have established ND adult communities.

🇪🇸 Spain

Adult ASD/ADHD diagnosis through the public system is slow (12–24 month waitlist); private assessment runs €600–1200. Catalonia, Madrid and Valencia have the strongest English-speaking ND clinical communities.

Source: Confederación Autismo España

🇮🇪 Ireland

HSE adult ASD/ADHD pathway exists but waitlists 18–36 months — most adults use private (€500–1500). AsIAm is the leading autism advocacy org with English-speaking community.

🇵🇹 Portugal

Adult ND diagnosis is improving but still limited — most expats use private clinics in Lisbon (€500–1000). FPDA and APPDA are the main support associations.

🇦🇷 Argentina

Adult ND diagnosis through public system slow but accessible; private psicólogos common and affordable (~ARS 15,000/session). Strong therapy culture; less ND-specific clinical infrastructure than EU.

Check carefully

Significant legal or practical gaps for this group.

🇮🇹 Italy

Adult ND assessment via SSN possible but waitlists exceed 18 months. Private clinics in Milan and Rome cost €400–800. Angsa is the main autism association.

🇵🇱 Poland

Adult ND services through NFZ are limited; private psychiatrists in Warsaw and Kraków charge PLN 250–500 per session. JIM Foundation supports autistic adults.

🇭🇺 Hungary

Adult ND services very limited via NEAK; private psychiatrists in Budapest €40–80/session. Smaller advocacy infrastructure than Western EU.

🇲🇽 Mexico

Adult ND diagnosis primarily private (~MXN 5,000–15,000). CDMX and Guadalajara have a growing ND-aware therapist community. Limited public infrastructure.
