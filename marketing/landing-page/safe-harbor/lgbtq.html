Safest Countries for LGBTQ+ Americans Moving Abroad

Marriage recognition, legal gender change, adoption rights and street-level acceptance vary enormously across the countries Americans relocate to most. Strong fit

Law, services and community all line up.

🇪🇸 Spain

Marriage equality since 2005 and a 2023 trans self-ID law. Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia, Sitges and Torremolinos rank among Europe's most queer-friendly cities; rural inland areas more reserved.

Source: ILGA-Europe Rainbow 2025

🇵🇹 Portugal

Marriage equality since 2010, full adoption rights, constitutional anti-discrimination on sexual orientation. Lisbon and Porto are highly inclusive; Algarve is welcoming due to large expat presence.

Source: ILGA-Europe Rainbow 2025

🇩🇪 Germany

Marriage equality since 2017. Berlin is one of the world's most queer-inclusive cities; Cologne, Hamburg, Munich all hold major Prides. 2024 Self-Determination Act allows legal gender change by self-declaration.

Source: ILGA-Europe Rainbow 2025

🇨🇦 Canada

Marriage equality since 2005. Conversion-therapy ban federal (2022). Toronto, Montreal, Vancouver, Ottawa all highly inclusive.

Source: ILGA Pan-American Equality Index: top-tier

🇮🇪 Ireland

Marriage equality since 2015 (first country by popular vote). Dublin, Cork and Galway are highly inclusive. Hate-crime legislation strengthened 2024.

Source: ILGA-Europe Rainbow 2025

Good, with caveats

Workable — read the note for the catch.

🇲🇽 Mexico

Marriage equality nationwide since 2022 (state-by-state rollout completed). Mexico City, Guadalajara and Puerto Vallarta highly inclusive; rural macho culture more conservative.

Source: ILGA Pan-American: high

🇦🇷 Argentina

Marriage equality since 2010 (first in Latin America). Gender Identity Law 2012 is among world's most progressive. Buenos Aires is one of LatAm's most queer-friendly cities; some federal protections under review by Milei government since 2024.

Source: ILGA Equality Index: high

🇬🇧 United Kingdom

Marriage equality since 2014 (E&W and Scotland) / 2020 (NI). Strong workplace protections via Equality Act 2010. UK Supreme Court 2025 ruling on sex/gender in Equality Act has narrowed trans protections; consult charity Stonewall for current state.

Source: ILGA-Europe Rainbow 2025

Check carefully

Significant legal or practical gaps for this group.

🇮🇹 Italy

Civil unions since 2016 but no marriage equality and no same-sex adoption. Rome, Milan, Bologna and Florence are inclusive; southern rural areas more conservative. The 2023 Meloni-era directive instructed municipalities to stop registering foreign same-sex parents on birth certificates.

Source: ILGA-Europe Rainbow 2025

🇵🇱 Poland

Civil partnerships introduced 2025 under Tusk government but no marriage or adoption rights. Several voivodeships repealed 'LGBT-free zone' declarations 2023–2024. Warsaw, Kraków, Poznań are safe and increasingly visible; small-town climate remains conservative.

Source: ILGA-Europe Rainbow 2025: 41/49 — middle-lower EU

🇭🇺 Hungary

2020 constitutional amendment defines family as man + woman; no marriage or adoption. 2021 'child protection' law restricts LGBTQ content visibility to minors. Budapest Pride still operates but faces 2025 restrictions.

Source: ILGA-Europe Rainbow 2025: 33/49 — among lowest in EU
