GoThere Access

A Plan B shouldn't depend on who can afford one

A lot of people are tired of watching their rights, their healthcare or their safety shift with every news cycle. Some of them want to know what moving abroad would really take. GoThere Access gives them the full app for free, and it's handed out by the nonprofits they already trust, not by us.

Status: launching November 2026, first partner nonprofits being invited now

How it works

A nonprofit asks for codes

Any organization serving LGBTQ+, trans, disabled, veteran, Black and POC, neurodivergent, senior, pregnant or single parent communities can ask. No fee, no contract.

They decide who gets one

The nonprofit hands codes to the people it serves. We never ask for names, case files or anything about the person receiving a code.

The member gets the full app

Every country, every visa path, the personal checklist and the Decide wizard that tailors the plan to their situation.

We report back

Partners and funders get a simple count: codes sent, codes redeemed. Totals only, never who redeemed them.

Who it's for

Each group has a free, sourced Safe Harbor guide that anyone can read without signing up. Access members get the app built on the same data.

What we promise

  • Every claim in the Safe Harbor guides links to a source. When something changes or we got it wrong, we fix it and say so.
  • We don't tell anyone to leave. We help people who are already asking the question get straight answers.
  • Partners choose who gets codes. We don't collect data about Access members beyond what any app user shares.
  • Funder money is reported openly: what it paid for and how many members it reached.
  • We are not lawyers. The app and guides point to official sources and to immigration lawyers when a decision needs one.
Where the data comes from. The Safe Harbor guides draw on ILGA equality indexes, ECRI country reports, government immigration and social security sources, the VA, and national disability and healthcare agencies, with a source on every line. See the full index.
