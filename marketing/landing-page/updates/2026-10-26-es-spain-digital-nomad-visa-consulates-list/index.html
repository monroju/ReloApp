Spain digital nomad visa: consulates list €2,442/month for 2026

Was: ~€2,650/mo
Now: €2,442/mo (consulate)

US-based Spanish consulates state 200% of the 2026 monthly minimum wage (€1,221 x 2 = €2,442). Applications filed inside Spain are often assessed at about €2,849 (annual SMI spread over 12 months). Family: +75% for the first member, +25% for each additional one.
