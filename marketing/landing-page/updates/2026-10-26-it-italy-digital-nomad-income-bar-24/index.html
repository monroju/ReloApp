Italy: digital nomad income bar ~€24,789/year; national visa fee €116

Was: DNV €28,000/yr, fee €300
Now: DNV ~€24,789/yr, fee €116

US consulates (Los Angeles, New York) state €24,789/year for the digital nomad visa. The national (type D) visa fee is €116; the €300 figure that circulates is the citizenship application fee.

[official source]

Verified 2026-10-26. Every figure links to its source.

Who this affects

  • Italy Digital Nomad Visa: highly skilled remote workers
  • Italy Elective Residence Visa: retirees and people living on passive income

Current requirements

Digital Nomad Visa

Income required
€24,789 (≈ $27,694)/year[official source]
Fees
National (type D) long-stay visa fee: €116 (≈ $130); National visa fee in USD at Consulate General Los Angeles (1 Oct to 31 Dec 2026): $134; Permesso di soggiorno: card production plus contribution for a permit up to 1 year: €70.46 (≈ $79); Revenue stamp (marca da bollo) for permesso application: €16.00 (≈ $18)
Processing
up to 120 days[official source]
Citizenship after
10 years[official source]

Elective Residence Visa

Income required
€31,000 (≈ $34,633)/year[official source]
Fees
National (type D) long-stay visa fee: €116 (≈ $130); National visa fee in USD at Consulate General Los Angeles (1 Oct to 31 Dec 2026): $134; Permesso di soggiorno: electronic card (smart card) production: €30.46 (≈ $34); Permesso di soggiorno contribution, permit up to 1 year: €40.00 (≈ $45); Revenue stamp (marca da bollo) for permesso application: €16.00 (≈ $18); Poste Italiane fee for sending the permesso kit: €30.00 (≈ $34)
Processing
up to 90 days[official source]
Citizenship after
10 years[official source]
