Portugal D7 and D8 income thresholds rise with the 2026 minimum wage

Was: D7 €870/mo, D8 €3,480/mo, fee €90
Now: D7 €920/mo, D8 €3,680/mo, fee €110

Minimum wage is €920/month in 2026 (Decreto-Lei 139/2025). D7 needs 1x (€920/month), D8 needs 4x (€3,680/month). National visa fee is €110.

[official source]

Verified 2026-10-26. Every figure links to its source.

Who this affects

  • Portugal D7 Visa: retirees and people living on passive income
  • Portugal D8 Digital Nomad Visa: remote workers for employers or clients outside Portugal
