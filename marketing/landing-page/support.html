Common questions

How do I delete my account and data?

Open the app menu and choose to delete your account. This removes your profile, your authentication credentials, and any documents you uploaded. If you cannot access the app, email us from the address on the account and we will do it for you.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Email us from your account address and ask for an export.

How do I unsubscribe from the newsletter?

Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any newsletter email, or email us.

How do I cancel a subscription or get a refund?

Subscriptions and purchases are billed by Apple or Google, not by us, so cancellations and refunds go through them. On iPhone, use Settings, then your Apple Account, then Subscriptions. On Android, use the Play Store, then Payments and subscriptions. We cannot issue refunds on their behalf, but tell us if something went wrong and we will help you sort it out.

Is the visa and citizenship information reliable?

We check it against official government sources and record the date the rules were last verified. It is still informational only, not legal advice, and rules change. Confirm with the relevant consulate or a qualified lawyer before you act. See our Terms of Service.

I think some country information is wrong.

Please tell us. Include the country, the screen, and a link to the official source if you have one. Corrections like this are genuinely useful and we act on them.

Which countries are covered?

Coverage varies by feature and grows over time. The citizenship-by-descent checker currently covers Ireland and Canada, with Italy, Poland, Germany, Portugal and the UK in progress. Where a country is not yet supported, the app says so rather than guessing.

Can I use GoThere through ChatGPT or Claude?

Yes. We publish a citizenship-by-descent tool for AI assistants at https://getgothere.app/mcp. It never asks for names and stores nothing.

Report a security issue

Email gabriel@getgothere.app with "security" in the subject line. Please give us a reasonable chance to fix an issue before disclosing it publicly.
